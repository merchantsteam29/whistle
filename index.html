How it works

Setting up

  1. Prop the phone or laptop up about waist high, 3–5 m away, so the player fits head to feet.
  2. Good light helps a lot. Avoid a bright window behind the player.
  3. One player in view at a time. It follows whoever it sees best.
  4. For auto-scored shots, put the camera off to the side so the hoop and the player are both in the picture, then mark the rim.
  5. Tap the ball once in setup. It learns that ball's colour, so any coloured ball works. White or grey balls are found by their movement instead.
  6. No court handy? Ref a recorded video in setup runs a clip from your phone through the ref, just like the camera.

The calls

TRAVELING

Too many steps holding the ball. Picked up on the move: 2 steps allowed (3 in Beginner). Picked up standing still: your first step sets the other foot as your pivot. Move the pivot and it's a travel.

DOUBLE DRIBBLE

You dribbled, picked the ball up, then started dribbling again. Once you pick it up, pass or shoot.

More calls

CARRYING

Mid-dribble the hand scoops under the ball and lifts it. You can turn this off in Settings for very young kids.

OUT OF BOUNDS

Mark the sideline in setup (two taps on the floor). A foot or a bounce past it with the ball is out.

3 SECONDS

Game mode: mark the paint, then nobody on offense may stand in it for more than 3 seconds.

SHOT CLOCK

Game mode: 14, 24 or 30 seconds to shoot. It resets on every shot and every change of possession.

Shooting form coach

Every shot gets a score out of 10 from four checks: legs (knees bend), elbow (an "L" under the ball), release (above the head) and follow-through (arm up and straight). You get one tip for the weakest part. The Coach dashboard tracks the average over time.

HORSE & Knockout

2 to 4 kids, one shooter in front of the camera at a time. In HORSE, make a shot and everyone has to copy it from the yellow spot; miss the copy and you get a letter. In Knockout, everyone shoots each round, and a miss when someone else made it knocks you out.

Highlights, dashboard and accounts

Makes, threes and 25-dribble streaks are saved as short clips with the WHISTLE overlay, and you can download them at the end of a session. The Coach dashboard shows each kid's progress over weeks. Sync uses your Game Library account so players, XP and badges follow you to other devices, and Leaderboards show today's best Handles 30 and Ten Shots scores.

Drills, challenges and plans

Drills: Handles 30, Ten Shots, Weak Hand 30, Crossovers 30, Between the Legs, Layup Lines and Defensive Slides, each with a personal best. Beat the Pro shows a ghost bar to race (the pro's score or your own best). Today's challenge is the same for everyone each day; finish it to keep your day streak going. Practice plans run a whole practice for you: stations, a rest countdown and voice cues.

Team mode and reports

Team mode runs a drill for a whole group: each kid gets a turn, their own XP, and a results table at the end with printable reports. The Parent report sums up a kid's last 7 or 30 days. Share it as a link (the numbers travel inside the link, nothing is uploaded) or print it.

Replay, scoreboard screen and arena

Replay plays the last few seconds in slow motion with the skeleton drawn on. Scoreboard turns a second phone or tablet into a big scoreboard: open the link it shows on the other device. Arena mode (Settings) adds a crowd, a buzzer and player introductions. Level up to unlock new scoreboard themes, whistles and celebrations on the Stats page.

Career, tricks and more shooting games

Career is a rookie season of 10 chapters with Coach Reyes: tryouts, drills, games against Riley (WHISTLE shoots for the rival) and a championship. The Trick Contest scores 30-second runs (crossovers, between the legs, spin moves, finishes, with combos), and three judges hold up cards. Shooting games now include 21 (2s and 3s, tip-ins worth 1, exactly 21 wins, over drops you to 13) and Around the World (make it from 5 spots in order). In 2v2 games WHISTLE counts passes, assists and steals.

Making the calls fairer

Calibrate (Settings) is a 1-minute setup per player: stand still, then dribble 5 times, and WHISTLE tunes its step and ball detection to them. If a call is wrong, tap Bad call: the replay clip and what the ref saw are saved on this device (Settings → Bad call reports) so they can be sent in for tuning. A side camera (a second phone filming from the side, link in the Scoreboard window) takes over travel calls, because steps are easier to see side-on.

Hands-free

Voice commands (Settings): say "start", "pause", "resume", "no call", "replay", "next", "made it", "missed" or "end session". The remote control link (in the Scoreboard window) turns another phone into big buttons for pause, no call, points and fouls. Leagues let friends in other houses compete on 3 drills of the week.

Offline and battery

Settings → Play offline downloads the tracking files once so WHISTLE works without internet. Battery saver tracks fewer frames, turns off replay and pauses when nobody's in view for a minute.

Game mode (1v1 / 2v2)

Everyone plays in front of one camera. HOME starts on the left, AWAY on the right, and WHISTLE keeps track of who is who (shirt colours help). Mark the hoop and the 3-point line in setup for automatic 2s and 3s.

REACH-IN

A defender's hand swipes into the ball handler's arm or body. Hitting the ball is a clean steal.

HOLDING

A hand stays on the other player's arm or body.

PUSHING

A hand on the body, then the other player gets shoved away. No travel is called on the player who got pushed.

OFFENSIVE FOUL

The player with the ball pushes off or holds the defender. The ball goes to the other team.

Fouls only count between players at the same distance from the camera, so standing behind someone isn't contact. A foul on a shot gives 2 free throws (3 behind the line). If the shot goes in anyway, it's an and-one. Team fouls past the bonus give 2 free throws, and a player who fouls out gives 2 free throws on every foul after that. The Ref button adds points or a foul by hand.

A ref can miss a call, and so can a camera. Tap No call to wave off a wrong one. Tap Fix shot to flip the last shot between made and missed.

Points

  • +1 XP every dribble, plus bonuses for streaks of 10, 25, 50, 100 and 200 in a row
  • +3 XP for every shot, +15 for a make, +5 extra for each make in a row
  • +4 XP for a clean pick-up: picked up, then passed, shot or dribbled with no violation
  • +25 XP for finishing a drill, +25 more for a new personal best
  • Violations don't take XP away. They end your streak.

Levels go Rookie → Bench → Sixth Man → Starter → Varsity → Captain → All-Conference → All-Star → MVP → Hall of Fame. There are badges to collect on the Stats page.

Privacy

All the tracking runs on this device. The video is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Progress is saved in this browser only.
